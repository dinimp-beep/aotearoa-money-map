# 🇳🇿 Aotearoa Money Map

### Thinking of moving to New Zealand? Know what life really costs before you pack your bags.

**Aotearoa Money Map** is a free, all-in-one guide for anyone planning to live, study or settle in New Zealand. It turns the big question, *"Can we actually afford to live here?"*, into a clear, city-by-city money plan.

Whether you're a fresh graduate, a young couple, a family with kids, an international student, a migrant worker or a retiree, it shows you what a normal month looks like in New Zealand. That covers rent, groceries, childcare, health insurance and tax, plus a little left over for adventure.

> 🎓 This website is part of the **Business Transformation** project class at **ICL Graduate Business School**, Auckland.

## 💡 Why it's different

- **Made for newcomers.** It covers the things people moving here actually worry about: student visa funds, school zones, the Healthy Homes standards, whether your kids pay international school fees, and what a "tax code" even is.
- **Real 2026 numbers.** Rents come from Trade Me and Tenancy Services data, and tax, NZ Super and minimum wage figures follow the official 2026–27 rates.
- **Plan in one click.** Turn any cost-of-living estimate, rent or trip into your own monthly budget.
- **Take it with you.** Download your simulation as a CSV or PDF to share with your partner, family or adviser.
- **Private by design.** No sign-up, no ads and no server. Everything you enter stays in your own browser.

---

## ⚠️ Good to know

All figures are **estimates based on late-2026 data** and are meant as a starting point for planning, not financial, tax, immigration or legal advice. Prices, visa rules and school zones change, so always check official sources such as IRD, Immigration New Zealand, Tenancy Services and Education Counts before making big decisions.
