      About these numbers

      Estimates are in NZD per month and reflect late-2026 conditions. Rents are typical asking rents for each city, based on the Trade Me Rental Price Index (August 2026: national median $625/week, Auckland $660, Otago $700, Canterbury $600, Wellington $600) and Tenancy Services bond data. Other lines are rounded benchmarks for a moderate lifestyle; your suburb, home size and habits will move them a lot. Always check official sources before making visa or money decisions.
